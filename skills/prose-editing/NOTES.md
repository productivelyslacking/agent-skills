# prose-editing: notes

Read on demand. Holds the examples behind the rules in `SKILL.md`, under its section headings.

## Cut

- **Restatement:** if a point appears in the intro and again in the steps, delete the intro copy.
- **Filler transitions** also include "keep in mind".
- **Editorializing:** "This is unglamorous but important" tells the reader nothing actionable.
- **Vague quantification:** "Resolves a surprising share of tickets" is filler.
- **Blockquotes:** one repeating a step louder is fluff.

## Protected: the *why* is never fluff

A runbook of bare steps produces someone who can follow it and is stuck the moment reality diverges.

| Cut | Keep |
|---|---|
| "This is unglamorous but it's right a surprising share of the time." | "This rules out half the problem space in one step." |
| "Astonishingly often, this is the whole answer." | "A broken home connection presents identically to a broken VPN." |
| "Getting it right the first time saves a second ticket." | *(no reason present - cut)* |

## Cut unearned structure

Ways a four-step procedure gets inflated to nine: splitting hairs, adding branches for cases that almost never occur,
wrapping a linear list in a decision tree it doesn't need.
