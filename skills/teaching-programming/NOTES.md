## Reading before writing

- PRIMM: better than control in 13 schools, ages 11 to 14, teacher-led [G-A2].
- Parsons problems: some students strongly dislike them [G-A3].
- Subgoal labels helped quizzes, not always exams, at-risk students most; low cost [G-A4].

## Errors and debugging

- Trial-and-error default: 17 students, ages 15 to 18 [G-A7].
- Debugging was among the skills AI use hurt most, in an exploratory subarea of one small trial [G-A7].
- The self-blaming character study found more levels finished in similar time [G-A6].

## Checkers

- Autograders mostly report pass or fail [G-A8].
- The one paper on penalising repeat runs was found without its results being read [G-A8].
- SCS1 is a validated language-independent CS1 test.

## Plans, order and prior languages

- 61% of 450 Stack Overflow posts (which over-sample confusion) had incorrect assumptions carried from a prior language, and
  learners had most trouble on false carryovers [G-A10].

## Programming games

- Nearest adult evidence: 60 paid crowd workers aged 18 to 41, randomised, developer-run, pseudo-code test. A debugging game matched
  Codecademy at about half the time (median 10 vs 12 of 24) [G-B8].
- After an estimated 5 to 10 hours, the best of the three conditions reached a median of 50% correct [G-B8].
